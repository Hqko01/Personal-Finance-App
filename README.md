# Personal Finance Tracker

A simple and intuitive personal finance tracking application built with Flutter.

## About the Project

Personal Finance Tracker aims to make personal financial management easier, more accessible, and more understandable.

Managing money shouldn't be complicated. Everyone should be able to understand where their money goes, track their spending habits, and make better financial decisions.

This project was created with the idea of turning financial awareness into a daily habit through a simple and user-friendly mobile experience.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals to take control of their financial lives.

We aim to provide a simple and accessible platform where users can track their income, monitor their expenses, understand their spending habits, and develop healthier financial behaviors.

We believe that financial awareness is the first step toward financial freedom.

## 🚀 Our Vision

Our vision is to make personal financial management accessible to everyone.

We want to build more than just an expense tracking application. We envision a platform that helps people understand their financial habits, plan their future, and make smarter decisions with their money.

Our long-term goal is to turn complex financial management into a simple, meaningful, and everyday experience.

## ✨ Core Features

- Income tracking
- Expense tracking
- Financial overview dashboard
- Spending analysis
- Transaction history
- Category-based expense management

*Features are subject to development.*

## 🛠️ Built With

- Flutter
- Dart

## 💡 Our Philosophy

**"Understand your money. Take control of your future."**

We believe that managing money should not require complicated tools or financial expertise.

Small steps, consistent tracking, and better awareness can make a meaningful difference in anyone's financial future.
